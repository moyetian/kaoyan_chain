# 考研英语通用作文功能句库

## 1. 图表/图画开篇描述
- As is vividly shown in the chart, there has been a noticeable change in...
- The data clearly reflects an upward / downward trend during the past few years.

## 2. 原因阐释与分析
- A variety of factors can account for this phenomenon, but the following are the most critical.
- First and foremost, with the rapid development of technology, people's lifestyle has transformed significantly.

## 3. 建议与展望收尾
- From what has been discussed above, it is imperative for us to take effective measures.
- Only in this way can we embrace a more promising future.
